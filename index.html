Verify against a known ring

This is the 6/60 Landolt C — the biggest ring we ever draw. It is only used for the practice ring, to check the calibration. The screening chart itself starts at 6/36. Its outer diameter is five units, the ring is one unit thick and the gap is one unit wide. At six metres the outer diameter must measure 87.3 mm, top to bottom. Measure it. If it is within about a millimetre, the calibration holds and the chart can be trusted.

Run the screening chart

Pick the sequence written on the child's code slip, hold up the correct colour occluder, then show all six lines in order, from the biggest rings (Line 1, 6/36) down to the smallest (Line 6, 6/6). Everyone in the group answers every line (if unsure, guess), about 30 seconds per line. When marking, a child's result for that eye is the smallest line read with 3 of 4 correct, working down from Line 1 and stopping at the first line not read. The child circles the matching arrow on their paper answer sheet — this screen never shows the answer, only the ring.
